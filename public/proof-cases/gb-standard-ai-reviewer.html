GB Standards AI Reviewer

An AI-assisted standards review concept for helping technical teams compare requirements, trace evidence and improve review discipline across safety-critical engineering contexts.

Case summary

Problem

Standards review is evidence-heavy

EHS and engineering teams need to retrieve, compare and apply requirements across GB, TB and HKRS contexts without losing clause meaning, source traceability or project context.

Build

Retrieval and semantic review support

The portfolio describes a vector-search and semantic-review assistant using LLM reasoning for standards interpretation. Structured prompts, source grounding and reviewer discipline keep the system pointed at evidence.

Value

Faster synthesis, clearer accountability

AI can reduce repetitive search and drafting while accountable technical professionals retain the final engineering judgement needed for safety-critical review.

What the reviewer is designed to support

Retrieve

Find the relevant requirement

Standards intelligence starts with locating the right source and clause across jurisdictions. A retrieval layer helps teams move from a broad question toward the technical material that needs review.

Compare

Make differences easier to inspect

Semantic search and LLM-assisted reasoning can support comparison notes, interpretation prompts and a clearer separation between source facts and explanation. Version context and citations should remain visible to reviewers.

Review

Keep compliance judgement accountable

Standards interpretation depends on definitions, scope, project conditions and regulatory expectations. The reviewer supports preparation; it does not decide compliance or replace qualified engineering judgement.

Why the review boundary matters

Standards work is a high-trust knowledge task because the same term can carry different consequences depending on scope, definitions, project conditions and the applicable jurisdiction. An AI reviewer therefore needs to make its source trail inspectable: retrieve the relevant material, show citations, distinguish fact from interpretation and keep uncertainty visible. A faster answer is not automatically a better review if the source, version or applicability is unclear. The engineering professional still decides whether the requirement applies and what action is justified. That boundary is part of the product logic, not a disclaimer added after the search experience.

Evidence

Retrieval support, not certification

The evidence supports a standards retrieval and semantic-review direction. No compliance certification, regulatory approval, guaranteed accuracy or independent audit result is claimed here.

Limitations

Human review remains required

AI may retrieve or draft, but accountable professionals must validate sources, context and conclusions. Safety Nexus does not promise autonomous compliance decisions.
