Diagnostic process

Step 1

Discovery call

Clarify the business context, operational risk profile, AI ambition and stakeholder expectations.

Step 2

AI safety readiness review

Review current workflows, data, governance, people capability, assurance routines and implementation constraints.

Step 3

Use-case and governance mapping

Prioritise use cases and define guardrails, ownership, evidence standards and approval logic.

Step 4

90-day pilot roadmap

Deliver a practical pilot plan with milestones, metrics, risks, communication rhythm and next investment logic.
