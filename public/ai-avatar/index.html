FROM CAMERA DAY TO CONTENT DAY

The message matters. The production should not get in the way.

A conventional executive video can turn a short message into a full production exercise. AI Avatar production removes the pressure of performing for the camera and replaces repeated filming with one controlled, reviewable workflow.

01

One short message becomes a production day.

Book a studio. Coordinate the crew. Prepare lighting, sound, wardrobe and teleprompter. Rehearse the delivery—or try to remember every line while staying natural on camera.

  • Every pause, mispronunciation or changed sentence can mean another take.
  • The strongest take still needs editing, captions, factual checks and approval.
  • A late correction can bring the speaker, crew and edit suite back into the process.

The hidden cost is not only the studio. It is everyone’s time.

02

One approved script becomes a repeatable digital performance.

Confirm the words, tone and brand style once. Your avatar delivers the message consistently—without camera nerves, forgotten lines or on-set “NG” moments.

  • No studio booking, camera crew, rehearsal day or repeated physical takes.
  • Captions, background and delivery stay consistent across every message.
  • If a line changes, revise the content without arranging another shoot.

One controlled production flow. Human-reviewed. Ready to publish.

CLIENT BRIEFING

What we need from you

A complete, approved brief keeps production fast and prevents avoidable regeneration costs.

  1. 01
    Final approved script

    Include correct names, titles, dates and pronunciation notes.

  2. 02
    Audience and tone

    Tell us who will watch and whether the delivery should feel formal, warm or energetic.

  3. 03
    Brand assets

    Supply your logo, brand colours, preferred background and any mandatory visual elements.

  4. 04
    Avatar, voice and format

    Choose language, voice, framing and 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1 output.

  5. 05
    Rights and consent

    You must own supplied assets and provide written consent for any custom likeness or voice.

  6. 06
    Deadline and consolidated feedback

    Confirm the delivery date and send revision notes in one clear list.
