CLIENT BRIEFING

What we need from you

A complete, approved brief keeps production fast and prevents avoidable regeneration costs.

  1. 01
    Final approved script

    Include correct names, titles, dates and pronunciation notes.

  2. 02
    Audience and tone

    Tell us who will watch and whether the delivery should feel formal, warm or energetic.

  3. 03
    Brand assets

    Supply your logo, brand colours, preferred background and any mandatory visual elements.

  4. 04
    Avatar, voice and format

    Choose language, voice, framing and 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1 output.

  5. 05
    Rights and consent

    You must own supplied assets and provide written consent for any custom likeness or voice.

  6. 06
    Deadline and consolidated feedback

    Confirm the delivery date and send revision notes in one clear list.
