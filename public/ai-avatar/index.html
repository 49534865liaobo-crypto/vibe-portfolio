FROM CAMERA DAY TO CONTENT DAY

The message matters. The production should not get in the way.

A conventional executive video can turn a short message into a full production exercise. AI Avatar production removes the pressure of performing for the camera and replaces repeated filming with one controlled, reviewable workflow.

01

One short message becomes a production day.

Book a studio. Coordinate the crew. Prepare lighting, sound, wardrobe and teleprompter. Rehearse the delivery—or try to remember every line while staying natural on camera.

  • Every pause, mispronunciation or changed sentence can mean another take.
  • The strongest take still needs editing, captions, factual checks and approval.
  • A late correction can bring the speaker, crew and edit suite back into the process.

The hidden cost is not only the studio. It is everyone’s time.

02

One locked production brief becomes a repeatable digital performance.

Validate the script, delivery and visual choices, then confirm “Approved to generate” in writing. Your avatar delivers the locked message consistently—without camera nerves, forgotten lines or on-set “NG” moments.

  • No studio booking, camera crew, rehearsal day or repeated physical takes.
  • Captions, background and delivery stay consistent across every message.
  • Refine the script and visual choices during the approval stage, before generation begins.

APPROVAL & CHANGE POLICY

Approve first. Generate once.

BEFORE GENERATION

We ask you to validate the final script, names, dates, pronunciation, language, avatar, voice, tone, timing, format, background, logo, captions, music and other visual elements.

THE APPROVAL GATE

Your written “Approved to generate” confirmation locks the brief. Production begins only after this approval is received.

AFTER GENERATION

Any client-requested change to wording, pronunciation, timing, avatar, voice, format, captions, background, branding, music or other elements requires a paid adjustment or regeneration.

Included: one consolidated pre-generation review. Additional rounds of client changes before approval may be quoted separately.

Fairness promise: if the delivered video does not match the approved brief because of our production error, we will correct that mismatch without an additional charge. New preferences, changed instructions and additional creative requests are not provider corrections.
