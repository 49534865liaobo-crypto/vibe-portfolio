Frequently asked questions

Who is the AI-Native EHS Starter Kit for?

It is for EHS, HSE, OSH, safety, risk and compliance professionals who want practical AI workflows, prompts and guardrails before buying larger advisory support.

Does the kit replace professional safety judgement?

No. The kit helps professionals use AI as a structured assistant while preserving human review, evidence checks and safety accountability.

What should I do after using the kit?

Create one visible artifact: a checklist, prompt workflow, safety campaign draft, standards note or AI governance mini-guide. That artifact becomes proof of practical AI capability.
